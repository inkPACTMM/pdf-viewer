About InkPact

A Youth-Led Digital Magazine Transforming Stories into Action

Our Story

InkPact Youth emerged from a shared vision among Myanmar's passionate young leaders who recognized the urgent need for authentic youth voices in sustainable development discourse. In a landscape where young people are often underrepresented despite being the primary drivers of change, we created a platform that not only amplifies these voices but transforms them into tangible action.

Our digital magazine serves as more than just a publication—it's a catalyst for community transformation. By aligning our storytelling with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals, we bridge the gap between awareness and action, ensuring that every story we share contributes to meaningful progress in our communities.

Our Mission

To create a secure, youth-led platform that amplifies voices from Myanmar's youth organizations while funding grassroots community projects through compelling storytelling aligned with UN Sustainable Development Goals.

We believe in the power of narrative to drive social change. Each article, interview, and feature story we publish is carefully crafted to not only inform but inspire action. Our unique model ensures that the impact extends beyond digital engagement—revenue from our magazine directly funds community projects proposed and led by young people across Myanmar.

Who We Serve

Our content reaches and resonates with a diverse audience committed to positive social change. From youth leaders organizing community initiatives to international organizations supporting development work, we create content that speaks to various stakeholders in the development ecosystem.

Educators and students find our stories valuable for understanding real-world applications of sustainable development principles, while policy makers gain insights into youth perspectives on critical issues. Civil society groups and international partners use our platform to understand grassroots movements and identify collaboration opportunities.

Our Commitment to Safety

Operating in a complex political environment requires unwavering commitment to contributor safety and editorial independence. We have implemented comprehensive security protocols that protect our contributors while maintaining the authenticity and impact of their stories.

  • 🔒 Security protocols for contributor anonymity when needed
  • ☁️ Safe digital hosting to avoid censorship
  • ⚖️ Legal advice for content related to activism
  • ✍️ Editorial independence and authenticity
This is an independent youth initiative not affiliated with any political entity. Our commitment is to the stories and voices of young people working toward a better future for Myanmar.
