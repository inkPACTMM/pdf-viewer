About InkPact

A Youth-Led Digital Magazine Transforming Stories into Action

Our Story

InkPact Youth emerged from a shared vision among Myanmar's passionate young leaders who recognized the urgent need for authentic youth voices in sustainable development discourse.

In a landscape where young people are often underrepresented despite being the primary drivers of change, we created a platform that not only amplifies these voices but transforms them into tangible action.

Our Mission

To create a secure, youth-led platform that amplifies voices from Myanmar's youth organizations while funding grassroots community projects through compelling storytelling aligned with UN Sustainable Development Goals.

Our Objectives

📢

Who We Serve

Our content reaches and resonates with a diverse audience committed to positive social change. From youth leaders organizing community initiatives to international organizations supporting development work.

Educators, students, policy makers, and civil society groups use our platform to understand grassroots movements and identify collaboration opportunities.
