About InkPact

A Youth-Led Digital Magazine Transforming Stories into Action

Our Story

inkPACT is a youth-led, impact-driven digital publication and storytelling platform based in Myanmar, committed to elevating the voices of young changemakers. We believe that every impactful idea begins with a single stroke of intention and through collaborative storytelling, we can spark movements, shift mindsets, and archive the momentum of youth in action.

Our Vision

To empower and amplify the voices of Myanmar youth by promoting sustainable development, climate action, gender equality, and access to education through innovative digital storytelling and inclusive publishing.

Our Mission

inkPACT strives to become a platform that documents, celebrates, and supports the progress of youth organizations across Myanmar. We aim to connect passionate changemakers, raise awareness about SDG goals, and reinvest our profits to uplift youth-led initiatives.

Our Objectives

📢

Digital Publishing

To publish a monthly digital magazine/newsletter featuring youth development stories, organizational activities, SDG goals, and educational resources.

🌍

Youth Engagement

To promote active youth engagement through storytelling and collaboration.

💰

Community Investment

To raise funds through magazine sales and reinvest them into youth-led community projects.

🌱

Safe Platform

To establish a safe, independent platform for youth to express, advocate, and connect.

Our Background

inkPACT began with a simple but urgent question: Why are so many powerful youth-led efforts overlooked? While volunteering and collaborating with youth organizations, we noticed something missing — not the passion or purpose, but the visibility. Many teams were building impactful projects, but their work rarely reached beyond their immediate circles. Their stories weren't being told, celebrated, or archived.

We believed that had to change. So we came together with a group of passionate youth from diverse creative and leadership backgrounds to build a platform that could connect, document, and amplify these efforts. Not just to spotlight achievements, but to create a ripple effect of recognition, trust, and collaboration.

That's how inkPACT was born — a digital bridge between passion and visibility, creativity and impact.

Core Activities of inkPACT

📖

Monthly Digital Magazine

Curated storytelling and insight-driven features spotlighting youth-led organizations, SDG efforts, and community voices across Myanmar.

🎓

Development Webinars & Workshops

Hosting skill-building sessions focused on career growth, digital marketing, civic engagement, and other professional development topics for youth and changemakers.

💬

Telegram Community Hub

A moderated space where youth organizations can share verified volunteer opportunities, calls for collaboration, and stay connected with like-minded peers.

📊

Annual Impact Review

Publishing a comprehensive, reflective report highlighting the real-world impact of our features, partnerships, and digital programs.
